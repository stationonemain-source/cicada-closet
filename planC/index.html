What to do

    Healing times vary with placement, size, colour and your own skin.

    When to see a doctor. Redness that spreads or gets worse after the first few days, pus, red streaks, or a fever are not part of normal healing. Get medical advice, then let Jenna know.

    The artist

    Jenna Feezel

    Jenna is a licensed tattoo artist with a Bachelor of Fine Arts, and she paints as well as tattoos. She works in colour and in black and grey. Moths, birds, beetles and botanicals turn up again and again.

    Her Instagram keeps highlights for healed work, booking, aftercare, and the designs she wants to tattoo next.

    Booking

    Start with a message.

    Message Jenna on Instagram at @jens.art. Her Booking highlight has her current details. A first message with these three things saves a round of questions:

    1. What you want, in a sentence, and any reference images.
    2. Where on the body, and roughly how big in inches.
    3. The days and times that work for you.
