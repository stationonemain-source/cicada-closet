One continuous shot, no cuts. Starting exactly from the reference frame: the hand-lettered logo on kraft paper on a walnut desk. The camera begins a slow, steady dolly push-in and tilt upward toward the small cicada at the top of the logo. As it closes in, the ink linework of the cicada fills the frame, the paper fibre becomes visible, and the tiny black keyhole on the cicada's back grows larger and larger until by the final second the keyhole fills the entire frame and the image is almost completely black with only a faint warm kraft rim at the edges. Identical colour grade throughout, warm muted daylight, no new objects, no text changes, no hands, photoreal macro, smooth motion.
