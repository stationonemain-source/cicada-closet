Preface

The Studio

The Cicada Closet is a female-owned tattoo studio on South Jenkins Avenue in Norman, Oklahoma. It opened in the summer of 2026 with Grillin’ & Chillin’, a grand opening and art show held on Saturday, the eleventh of July.

Its owner, Jenna Feezel, is a licensed tattoo artist who also holds a Bachelor of Fine Arts. Her tattoo work and her paintings share the same subjects, which is why this guide files them side by side.

Local Artist Call. The studio keeps a small gallery wall for local artists. Artists keep 100% of their sales: no commissions, no fees, just community.

Appendix A

On Booking

Appointments begin with a message to Jenna on Instagram at @jens.art. Her Booking highlight there carries her current details.

A first message that includes the following saves a round of questions:

  1. What you would like, in a sentence, with any reference images.
  2. Where on the body, and roughly how large, in inches.
  3. The days and times that suit you.

Appendix B

On Aftercare

Follow the instructions Jenna gives you; her Aftercare highlight on Instagram has her version. What follows are the general basics most artists agree on.

The first night

Leave the wrap on for as long as you were told. When it comes off, wash gently with lukewarm water and unscented soap, then pat dry with a clean paper towel.

The first fortnight

Wash twice a day and apply a thin layer of whatever product your artist recommends. Thin means you can barely see it.

Leave it be

No picking and no scratching. Showers are fine; baths, pools, lakes and hot tubs wait until it has fully healed.

Out of the sun

Keep it out of direct sun while it heals. Afterwards, sunscreen is what keeps fine lines fine.
